## Nour El Nil

- **Site:** https://www.nourelnil.com/
- **Ranking keywords (observed / likely):** luxury dahabiya Nile cruise; Esna to Aswan dahabiya; Nour El Nil Dendera; panoramic suite dahabiya. Strong brand + “authentic dahabiya” modifiers. Less “Luxor to Aswan Nile cruise” because their classic run is Esna–Aswan (5 nights / 6 days).
- **Page strategy:** Fleet-first. Each boat is a URL (`/boats/dendera/`, `/boats-el-nil/`, Adelaide, etc.). Shared itinerary block (Luxor/Aswan, 5 nights) repeated per vessel. Cabin types (Luxury Room / Panoramic Suite) live on the boat page, not as separate commercial URLs.
- **Content gaps for Hathor:** Directional itineraries (Luxor→Aswan vs Aswan→Luxor) as indexable pages; Royal Suite as its own URL; private charter as a dedicated service page; journal supporting those commercials. Nour El Nil under-serves “3-night Aswan to Luxor” and “Royal Suite Nile Cruise”.
- **Title patterns:** `[Boat name] | Nour El Nil` and `Nour El Nil | Luxury Nile Egypt Dahabiya Cruise | [Boat]`. Brand-first, boat-second. Short on keyword-led CTR.
- **Schema usage:** Limited public JSON-LD on boat pages (primarily Open Graph). FAQ appears on OTA copies, not consistently on the brand site.
- **Internal linking:** Homepage → fleet → individual boats. Weak voyage-direction silos. Charter is mixed into boat booking rather than a hub.
- **Hathor counter:** Keep `/voyages/luxor-to-aswan` and `/voyages/aswan-to-luxor` unique. Do not copy their Esna-centric copy. Own “twelve guests / Royal Suite / private charter” specificity.

## Ashranda

- **Site:** https://www.ashranda.com/
- **Ranking keywords (observed / likely):** Ashranda dahabiya; Nawara Dahabiya; Nuun Nuut Nile cruise; boutique hotel + cruise bundles. Brand and vessel names dominate over generic “luxury dahabiya Nile cruise”.
- **Page strategy:** Hospitality group IA. `/nile-cruises/`, `/hotels/`, then vessel pages (`/nawara-dahabiya/`, `/nuun/`). FAQ blocks on several templates (real on-page Q&A). Mixes hotels with cruises — dilutes cruise-only authority.
- **Content gaps for Hathor:** Pure-play Dahabiya story; Royal Suite product page; editorial journal; charter page with service schema. Ashranda’s FAQ is a gap Hathor should only fill where FAQs actually exist on-page.
- **Title patterns:** `[Vessel] – Ashranda`. Homepage “Art of local hospitality” is brand, not query.
- **Schema usage:** WordPress FAQ-style content present; organisation schema inconsistent across templates.
- **Internal linking:** Group nav (Hotels / Nile Cruises / Dahabiyyat / Restaurants). Cruise equity leaks to hotels.
- **Hathor counter:** Stay vessel-singular. Footer and homepage should keep pouring authority into Voyages → routes, Charter, Suites.

## Dahabiya.net

- **Site:** https://dahabiya.net/ (and related OTA-style listings)
- **Ranking keywords (observed / likely):** dahabiya (generic); dahabiya Nile cruise; cheap/compare dahabiya. Domain-keyword advantage on the head term “dahabiya”.
- **Page strategy:** Marketplace / listing. Multiple boats as cards. Titles often `[Boat] Dahabiya Nile Cruise`. Thin overlapping descriptions.
- **Content gaps for Hathor:** Luxury positioning, original photography, unique Royal Suite and charter pages, journal depth. Marketplace pages rarely earn trust for high-ticket private charter.
- **Title patterns:** Keyword-stuffed `Dahabiya Nile Cruise` repeats. CTR is functional, not luxury.
- **Schema usage:** Product/Offer markup common on listing themes; quality varies (incomplete prices, mixed vessel types).
- **Internal linking:** Filter → boat. Little storytelling silo.
- **Hathor counter:** Never stuff “dahabiya” into every title. Win on luxury modifiers + route + suite type. Use Organisation + BoatTrip, not fake Product offers.

## Sonesta Amirat (Dahabeya Amirat / Amirat II)

- **Site:** https://www.sonesta.com/sonesta-dahabeya-amirat-ii (and Sonesta Cruises)
- **Ranking keywords (observed / likely):** Sonesta Dahabiya; Sonesta Amirat Nile cruise; 4 night Aswan to Luxor dahabiya; butler Nile cruise. Brand + hotel-chain trust.
- **Page strategy:** Hotel-chain template: ship overview, sail dates, itinerary, FAQ (real on-page questions: inclusions, booking, docking). Two 4-night directions on one ship page rather than two URLs.
- **Content gaps for Hathor:** Separate directional URLs; Hathor-specific Royal Suite (56 m²); independent journal; private full-ship charter page. Sonesta FAQ is a model — only replicate if Hathor pages actually show those answers.
- **Title patterns:** `Nile River Cruise with Sonesta Dahabeya Amirat II`. Brand + vessel, weak generic “Luxor to Aswan Nile Cruise” targeting.
- **Schema usage:** FAQPage likely on the Sonesta template; organisation inherited from sonesta.com.
- **Internal linking:** Sonesta Cruises fleet (larger ships + dahabiya). Amirat equity competes with motor ships.
- **Hathor counter:** Smaller, quieter, twelve-guest story. Directional landing pages Sonesta has not split.

## Merit Dahabiyas

- **Site:** https://www.meritdahabiya.com/ and https://meritdahabiyas.com/
- **Ranking keywords (observed / likely):** Merit Dahabiya; Merit I / Merit II Nile cruise; Saturday Luxor–Aswan 5 nights; charter dahabiya Egypt.
- **Page strategy:** Split properties (older ASP site + newer `meritdahabiyas.com`). Day-by-day itineraries on boat pages. Charter called out. Duplicate domains are a cannibalisation risk for *them*.
- **Content gaps for Hathor:** Single canonical domain (EasyTravEgypt), consistent metadataBase, sitemap, and no competing www/non-www boat microsites. Merit’s day-by-day copy is stronger than most — Hathor route pages should eventually add unique itinerary body (without redesigning the voyages visual system).
- **Title patterns:** `Dahabiya Merit | Luxury Charter Nile Cruise Experience`. Mixes charter + boat in one title (intent clash).
- **Schema usage:** Sparse on the legacy ASP site.
- **Internal linking:** Phone/email heavy; weak HTML silos.
- **Hathor counter:** Do not mix charter into Royal Suite titles (Merit-style clash). Keep `/charter` and `/royal-suites` separate.

## Oberoi (Zahra / Philae)

- **Site:** Oberoi Hotels Nile cruise pages (Zahra, Philae)
- **Ranking keywords (observed / likely):** Oberoi Nile cruise; luxury Nile cruise Egypt; Oberoi Philae; Oberoi Zahra. Dominates **luxury Nile cruise** (motor yacht, not dahabiya).
- **Page strategy:** Hotel-brand luxury. Spa, dining, suite types as hotel rooms. Itineraries 5–7 nights Luxor–Aswan. Very strong domain authority.
- **Content gaps for Hathor:** “Dahabiya vs luxury Nile cruiser” journal (already in Hathor posts — point those articles at `/voyages` and `/charter`). Hathor should not bid on “Oberoi” terms. Own **dahabiya** + **private** + **twelve guests**.
- **Title patterns:** `[Ship] | The Oberoi [Ship], [Destination]`. Suite pages as hotel rooms.
- **Schema usage:** Hotel / LodgingBusiness / HotelRoom — mature.
- **Internal linking:** Hotels.com-class: ship → rooms → dining → spa → offers.
- **Hathor counter:** Mirror the *silo* (voyages / cabins / suites / spa / dining) without copying hotel language. HotelRoom schema on suite/cabin pages is the right equivalent.

## Sanctuary / Abercrombie & Kent

- **Site:** Sanctuary Retreats Nile (Sun Boat, Nile Adventurer) and A&K Egypt journey pages
- **Ranking keywords (observed / likely):** Sanctuary Nile cruise; Abercrombie Kent Egypt; luxury Egypt tour Nile. Package/tour intent more than vessel intent.
- **Page strategy:** Journey + ship. Heavy itinerary PDFs, departure dates, suite names. A&K often ranks for “luxury Egypt tour” not “dahabiya”.
- **Content gaps for Hathor:** Direct-book dahabiya charter; independent travellers (not only tour packages); Egypt journal with commercial links. Hathor is not a tour operator mega-brand — win intimacy and private charter.
- **Title patterns:** `[Journey name] | Abercrombie & Kent` / `Sanctuary [Ship]`.
- **Schema usage:** TouristTrip / Product on package pages.
- **Internal linking:** Destinations → journeys → ships. Strong, but package-first.
- **Hathor counter:** BoatTrip + Service (charter), not generic TouristTrip packages unless Hathor sells the same product type.

## Viking

- **Site:** https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/ (Egypt / Nile itineraries)
- **Ranking keywords (observed / likely):** Viking Nile cruise; Viking Egypt; Nile river cruise. Massive domain authority on **Nile cruise** head terms. Not a dahabiya competitor; it defines the generic SERP Hathor must differentiate from.
- **Page strategy:** Industrial: itinerary URLs, dates, ships, FAQs, brochure. Excellent technical SEO (sitemaps, hreflang, schema).
- **Content gaps for Hathor:** Everything Viking cannot be: wind-assisted, 12 guests, Royal Suite, private charter, quiet moorings. Journal posts “How is a Dahabiya different from a Nile cruise?” must keep linking to `/voyages` and `/charter`.
- **Title patterns:** `[Nights] [Name] | Viking` / `Egypt Nile River Cruise`.
- **Schema usage:** Extensive (Cruise, Offer, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Organization).
- **Internal linking:** Relentless: itinerary ↔ ship ↔ dates ↔ destinations.
- **Hathor counter:** Do not compete on “Nile river cruise” head term. Compete on **Luxury Dahabiya Nile Cruise**, directional dahabiya routes, and private charter.
